Builders and renovation specialists in North London

Yordan Decorations & Building Ltd is a fully insured building and decorating company based in North London (N8). Founded by Yordan Minchev, the team brings over 15 years of hands-on experience to home renovations, kitchen and bathroom refurbishments, loft conversions, extensions and structural works across London.

Painters, decorators and property maintenance

Our painters and decorators handle interior and exterior painting, wall preparation and finishing. For day-to-day upkeep we offer plumbing, electrical work, boiler and heating repairs, windows and doors, double glazing, deep cleaning, garden and waste clearance and a reliable handyman service.

Landlords, HMOs and commercial properties

We help landlords and HMO operators keep properties let-ready with repairs, tenancy turnaround, decorating and planned maintenance. For businesses we deliver commercial fit-outs and maintenance for restaurants, shops and offices, often working around trading hours. For urgent problems, call {{PHONE}} for emergency repairs.

Meet Yordan Minchev: Craftsmanship, Commitment, and 15+ Years of Proven Reliability

With over 15 years of hands-on experience across the UK and Europe, Jordan has built a reputation grounded in exceptional quality, versatility, and an unwavering work ethic.

Originally bringing a rich heritage of traditional craftsmanship and dedication from Turkey, Jordan has dedicated his career to mastering every corner of the building and handyman trades. From intricate plumbing repairs and complex electrical setups to custom tinkering, full renovations, and structural maintenance, Jordan’s all-around expertise ensures that every project—big or small—is handled with precision.
