1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website can store on your device. “Similar technologies”, such as browser session storage, work in a comparable way. Websites use them to work properly, remember choices or, in some cases, track visitors.

2. The short version

This website does not set advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or any similar tool. Because of this, we do not show a cookie consent banner. If we add any non-essential cookies in future, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

4. Third-party content

  • Google Fonts. The site loads its fonts from Google (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Your browser sends your IP address and browser details to Google to receive the fonts. Google states that its Fonts service does not set cookies. See Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Hosting. Our hosting provider, Vercel, receives technical request data (such as IP address and browser type) in server logs so the site can be delivered and kept secure.
  • Links to other sites. Links to TikTok, WhatsApp and Google Maps are normal links. Nothing from those services is loaded on our pages. Once you click through, their own cookie and privacy policies apply.
  • Quote form. When you submit the quote form, your details are sent to our form delivery provider (FormSubmit) so they can be emailed to us. This is not a cookie. See our Privacy Policy.
