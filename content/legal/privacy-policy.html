1. What information we collect

When you ask for a quote or contact us

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • The postcode of the property, the type of property, the service you need and the description of the work you give us.
  • Anything else you choose to send us by phone, email or WhatsApp, including messages and photos of your property.

When you become a customer

  • Your address, access details, quotes, invoices, payment records and correspondence about the job.

When you visit the website

  • Basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device and the pages requested. This is recorded by our hosting provider in server logs.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers. See our Cookie Policy.

We do not knowingly collect “special category” data (such as health information). Please do not include it in your enquiry unless it is needed for the job, for example access or safety needs.

3. Who we share your information with

We only share what is needed, and only with:

  • Website hosting: Vercel Inc., which hosts this website and keeps standard server logs.
  • Quote form delivery: when you submit the quote form, FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) receives your details and passes them to us by email.
  • Email and communications: Google (Gmail) for email; WhatsApp (Meta) if you message us there.
  • Font delivery: Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads so that it can send the fonts.
  • Subcontractors and suppliers working on your job, where they need your contact or site details.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants and insurers, and authorities such as HMRC where the law requires.

These providers act on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own privacy policies. Links on our site to TikTok, WhatsApp and Google Maps take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

4. Transfers outside the UK

Some of the providers above are based in or use servers in the United States or elsewhere outside the UK. Where this happens we rely on appropriate safeguards required by UK law, such as the UK–US data bridge, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep your information

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Customer and job records: up to 6 years after the job ends, which covers tax and accounting requirements and the time limit for most contract claims. We may keep records longer where needed for insurance, guarantee or legal claims.
  • Website server logs: kept by our hosting provider for a short period under its own retention settings.

6. Keeping your information safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including secure (HTTPS) connections on this website and access controls on our email and devices. No internet transmission is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive details such as bank or card numbers by email or through the quote form.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • be told how we use your information and ask for a copy of it (access);
  • have inaccurate information corrected;
  • ask us to delete your information, in some circumstances;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it, in some circumstances;
  • object to our use of it where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • receive information you gave us in a portable format, where we use it under contract or consent; and
  • withdraw your consent at any time where we rely on it. This does not affect use before you withdrew.

To use any of these rights, email yordandecoandbuilding@gmail.com or call 07846 292529. We will normally reply within one month. We may need to check your identity first.

Complaints. Please contact us first so we can try to put things right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113.

8. Photos of your property and our work

We may take photos or videos while working on a project, for example to record progress, for insurance, or to show our work. We will only use images that identify you or your home (for example in our portfolio, on our website or on TikTok) with your permission. Tell us at any time if you would like an image removed.

9. Children

Our website and services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be shown on this website.

11. Contact us

For any question about this policy or your information, contact us using the details above.
